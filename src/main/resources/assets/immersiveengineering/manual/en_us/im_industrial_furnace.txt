<&industrial_furnaces>
The Industrial Furnace is a multiblock machine capable of fast and bulk smelting.
Build the structure as shown above, then right-click the front bottom Light Engineering Block with a hammer. However, you also need to install coils before it can operate.<np>
<&coil>
<&industrial_furnaces_demo>
Taking copper coils as an example.<np>
Upgrading the coils reduces total energy consumption and increases processing speed.
After building the coils, you must right-click the center of any side of the coil with an Engineer's Hammer to form it.
The Industrial Furnace can process all furnace and blast furnace recipes, as well as some special recipes, but it cannot process food.
