The Small Mining Machine is a machine that rapidly excavates ores from underground.
Build the structure as shown above, then right-click the front Steel Scaffolding with a hammer.<np>
The Small Mining Machine requires a Drill Head to operate. It scans and excavates minerals from its own chunk and surrounding chunks, with the range depending on the drill's mining area.

You can also install some drill-compatible upgrades:
Rock Melting Acid - increases mineral drops
Auger - increases mining speed
Advanced Lubrication System - reduces drill wear
