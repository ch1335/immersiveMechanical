Greenhouse
The "Greenhouse" Effect!
<&multiblock>The Greenhouse is a large machine capable of rapidly growing crops.
Build the structure as shown above, then right-click the front Light Engineering Block with a hammer.<np>
The Greenhouse can handle one fluid type and four different soil types at once, with each soil holding four seeds, for a total of 16 crops.
Crops in the Greenhouse grow 25% faster than in a Garden Cloche, with 3x fertilizer efficiency and 1.5x fluid efficiency.
You can input fluids from the left and right sides, input fertilizer from the rear, and all outputs are automatically sent to the container in front.<np>
Energy must be input through the center block on the top.
Note that this machine will NOT stop working even if the output buffer is full! All excess outputs will be wasted and resources will continue to be consumed.
