Large Battery
Even More Capacity!
<&multiblock>The Large Battery is used to store vast amounts of energy.
Build the structure as shown above, then right-click the front bottom Steel Plate Metal with a hammer to create it.<np>
This multiblock requires a Large Battery Core placed in the center to store and release energy. All energy is stored within the Large Battery Core.
<&large_battery_core>.
By default, a Large Battery Core can store 64MFE of energy with no input or output power limits.
The Large Battery Core retains its energy when broken.
The Large Battery can only receive energy from the top-left and output from the top-right.
