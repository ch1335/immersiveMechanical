The Pyrolyse Oven is an industrial device that heats organic materials under oxygen-free conditions to decompose them into combustible liquids/gases and solid residues.
Build the structure as shown above, then right-click the front-left Heavy Engineering Block with a hammer to form it.
<&coil_vertical>
Like the Industrial Furnace, you also need to install coils before it can operate. However, this device requires two sets of coils in a vertical configuration, and both coil sets must be of the same type.
<&pyrolyse_oven_demo>
Taking copper coils as an example.<np>
By default, the Pyrolyse Oven has 4x the parallel processing of a Coke Oven under the same conditions, and operates 25% faster than a Coke Oven.
